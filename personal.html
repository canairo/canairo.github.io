personal

here is a personal page of some books i have read and some books i would like to read, as well as some things i like. i am keeping this page purposefully austere, and if i wished to lie to you i would say that this is a design decision informed by the spread of ugly, ugly AI webpages, with their miasmatic blue glows, pale accents, hideous serifs. but in reality, i just like the stripped-back look, and this is less effort for me to develop.

in progress
  1. notes of a crocodile qiu miaojin (thanks to saint for recommending. honestly, never a bad recommendation. i enjoyed heaven and convenience store woman a lot. it's nice that the things that have happened to this taiwanese lesbian in the 90s have also happened to me, and by extension, everyone on earth. the power of books is in their ability to ascribe simple sentences to all these terrible things that we all seem to universally experience at our own time. there are maybe only three ever heartbreaks in the entire span of the universe, and everyone in all of human history just keeps shuffling through each of them. this is the pattern we are all locked in, we do this over and over until we die. to some people this may be a terribly depressing fact, but there is a lot of solace to be found here: it is absurd to think my heartbreaks or pains are especially difficult, painful, or complicated. when the pain of my past feels insurmountable, there is profound comfort in knowing that billions of people have overcome that exact same heartbreak. that's sort of all we do as human beings.)
