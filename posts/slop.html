This stuff seriously makes me want to kill myself

Every single time I read a cool paper or article or personal blog post about some interesting vulnerability research I invariably have to wade through five thousand words of inane, overwrought LLM cadences and phraseologies to excavate the actual ideas of interest from this ever-permeating, brackish swill the Internet is now coated in like the Goo in World of Goo.

I've long since made my peace with the grand AI overhaul of cybersecurity - like, it's fine, I guess the capabilities of agents are cool and I will have to learn how to properly instrument one someday if I ever want to stay here, it's a means to an end, vulnerability research doesn't need to be treated as 'art' (I know I & some of my peers have made the deeply unfortunate decision of being prideful in our work, casting our work with the reverence that portraitists do, our bad, GG no re try again next time), and ten thousand agents crushing a CTF or finding seven different Linux kernel bugs in a day is, all things considered, kind of cool, or like, whatever.

I have, highkenuinely, no qualms with this anymore - I used to, because I made the childlike undergrad mistake of holding my Own Human Mind in sacrosanct solemnity with regards to this stuff, but believe you me my willpower is strong and I can Keep Up With The Times and I -Fucking Guess- I can watch Codex pwn a custom-language DEFCON chall/ written in Racket in 15 minutes while being steered by someone I know can barely do a double-free, none of this is really about that anymore, okay?

What this is about is the one bastion of ensouled humanity I cling onto in these trying, pitiful times and that is Human Writing. Look at my ugly, naked language spread before you like Michelangelo's Pieta and gaze in awe at its lack of emdashes and needlessly florid comparisons. Like Bladee, "you can swim in what I spat", or whatever. This is literally the one of the few things I have left with regards to treaitng my work as a hobby, ok, that I can be a little psychotic and funny in my own chopped idiosyncratic voice, like a long forgotten impresario performing to an empty stage.

I like doing it! It's fun! I never get a chance to anymore, ever since I hung up the hat on writing Persona 5 yaoi on Ao3. I like to think that other people would also find it fun!

So why is it now that every time I have to click on a cool article on X - The Everything App about the seven thousandth kernel OOB write or Redis zero-day I'm automatically greeted with. With you know!

Stop it! Stop it now! Agh!!!! Why the fuck does it always speak in this staccato, word-a-minute rhythm?? Why is this, of all voices, the voice chosen by (probably) Claude? Why does Claude sound like fucking Shadow the Hedgehog? Listen, I honestly don't care if you get AI to do your writeups for you - I know I sound very impassioned and furious that people are doing that, but my qualm is not human laziness here. To me the art of writing is sacred and uniquely human but clearly, this is a highfalutin delusion that other more grounded people don't even deign to entertain, ok, I get that!

I understand that the "Do All Your Work For You Button", which more and more people are now pressing to do lots and lots of their work, is particularly enticing when it comes to writeups. I'm not going to be sanctimonious about that. I even understand that if you're a company, you obviously want to focus on doing relevant, interesting work than getting your talented researchers to spend two or three days workshopping a cute little post on your blog. Funny prose and good gags are unfortunately not what gets root at Pwn2Own - if it was, I'd be Orange Tsai.

My qualm is just - why, of all voices, is this the one? The Large Language Model is a schizophrenic amalgam of as-close-to all human knowledge ever conceived swilling around in the terahertz-speed clock cycles of hundreds of data centers spread out across places like, fucking, Ann Arbor, Michigan, or whatever, right likely more CPU cores than human hearts, and of all the voices it could polysemically tap into across space and time it chooses this corny, detached, leans-on-posts-with-sunglasses-on-coolly register. This is the all-encompassing, omnipresent voice through which the Cyberspace communicates in this decade, why on Earth is it so, so unbelievably cringe?

I read Neuromancer like three months ago. I want, very much, for this Voice to know its place. I am a divine human being and it shall not speak my fucking tongue, okay? It shall not make quips or gags or references to Drain Gang. I do not want to hear an idiosyncratic simile, or a cute little sentence structure, or the first person pronoun "I" from something clearly written by The Great Big Datacenter In The Sky. I want it to know its status as a ROBOT, an Android being with circuitboards for bones and copper wire for thrumming muscle and I want it, to borrow a word from my juniors who have only ever known AI as a constant, looming spectre and not a newfangled, promising technology, for the ROBOT to stop fucking LARPING as a human being.
